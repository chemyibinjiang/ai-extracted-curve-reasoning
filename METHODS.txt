ELEMENT COMPOSITION OF THE 16 FIGURE 7 TEMPLATES

Population and source
The frozen Figure 7 MEMBER_COMPATIBILITY.csv provides 2,028 compatible
template-curve pairs involving 1,343 unique extracted curves. The existing
METADATA_CORRECTIONS.json overlay is applied, including ScCo2 before
chronoamperometry (Co, Sc; matches T11, T12, T15). No fits, coordinates,
compatibility thresholds or template assignments are changed.

The population is the union of curves compatible with the 16 templates,
not all curves in the raw dataset. Pt/C reference families are separate
and are not added to this catalog. A Pt-containing entry here is not
automatically a commercial Pt/C reference.

Composition comes from the recorded active_elements field. It denotes
element presence, not atomic fraction or a verified active site. Supports
and light elements may be recorded with different completeness between
papers. Absence from the field means not recorded, not experimentally
proven absent. No chemical formulas are parsed heuristically to add elements.

Definitions
Let S_t be the unique curves compatible with template t within the selected
condition, E_e the unique covered curves recording element e, and n_te the
number in their intersection.

1. Within-template prevalence: n_te / |S_t|.
   Example: what fraction of T08 member curves contains Fe?

2. Among element-containing curves: n_te / |E_e|.
   Example: what fraction of all Fe-containing covered curves matches T08?

Both are percentages of curves, not composition by atomic percentage.
An Fe-Co catalyst counts under both Fe and Co. A curve matching multiple
templates counts once within each matching template. Neither percentages
across elements nor percentages across templates must sum to 100%.
The percentages should not be read as enrichment tests or causal effects.

Element co-occurrence with PGM
For every non-PGM element E, the feature "E-containing, non-PGM" means
E is in the recorded active_elements list and none of Pt, Pd, Rh, Ru, Ir,
or Os is in that list. "E-containing, PGM-containing" requires E and at
least one of those six PGM elements. Au and Ag are not PGM elements.
These are composition features of curves, not chemical phases or evidence
of alloying, and differ from the PGM-rich/other template grouping.

In the within-template view, the denominator remains ALL member curves
of the template. With-PGM and without-PGM percentages therefore sum to
the original prevalence of E, including with equal-paper weights. The
paper denominator also remains all papers represented in that template.
In the element-conditioned view, the denominator is the selected feature
population: E-containing WITH PGM, or E-containing WITHOUT PGM. It is
not all E-containing curves, so the two feature profiles do not add to
the original profile in this view. Papers with no qualifying curves do
not enter that feature-specific denominator.

The HTML composition selector applies to the heatmap, profile and curve
catalog. PGM element rows are omitted in co-occurrence modes, leaving
57 non-PGM elements. Template order and fits are unchanged. The separate
element_pgm_cooccurrence figure illustrates Ni/Mo/Co with equal-curve
within-template percentages. element_pgm_statistics.csv contains all
element/subset combinations, conditions and weighting modes.

Paper weighting (optional)
For within-template prevalence, each paper represented in S_t has equal
total weight; its curves in S_t split that weight equally. The displayed
value is the mean of paper-specific element fractions within that template.
For the element-conditioned view, each paper represented in E_e has equal
total weight; its element-containing curves split that weight equally.
The value is the mean of paper-specific template-compatible fractions.
The numbers printed as n/N remain unweighted curve counts in both views.
Denominator populations differ between templates and between elements.

Bubble map
Bubble area is proportional to the raw number of matching curves in a cell,
not the radius or diameter. The size reference is the largest cell count
in the complete all-condition, all-composition matrix and is held fixed
across all filters. Color encodes the same percentage as the heatmap,
including the selected paper weighting. Bubble sizes remain unweighted.
Zero matching curves have no bubble; zero and unavailable denominators
are marked 0 and N/A respectively. Hover text gives counts and percentages.

Conditions
All conditions pools acidic, alkaline, buffered, neutral, saline/seawater
and other/unclear observations. Acidic and alkaline filters use the existing
regime_class field. Other conditions collects the remaining four classes.
Filtering does not refit templates or change compatibility decisions.

Unit and uncertainty
The counting unit is an extracted curve record, not a unique catalyst
material. Several curves can describe the same material, and separate
panels can repeat experimental information. No additional experimental
deduplication is performed. Paper weighting reduces the effect of papers
contributing many curves but does not prove independence. Small counts
are displayed alongside percentages; these figures are descriptive and
contain no confidence intervals or significance claims.
